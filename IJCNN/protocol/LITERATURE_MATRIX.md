# IJCNN literature comparison — initial working matrix

Status: scoped comparison based on the discussed texts; not an exhaustive literature search or proof of novelty. Exact implementation details should be checked before reproducing a paper. Date: 21 November 2026.

| Reference | Target / data | Graph and method | Main lesson | Limitation or action for our study |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Zhang et al., [GNN-HAR preprint](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2308.01419), final publication [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ijforecast.2024.09.002) | Intraday-derived individual-stock realized volatility; US stocks. | GLASSO-derived relationships, HAR / graph HAR / nonlinear GNN, multiple hops and losses. | Multi-hop information alone does not guarantee gains; training loss and horizon matter. | Verify final versus preprint; daily close proxy is not the same target. Use strong multiscale baselines. |
| Gorduza et al., [GAE market instability](https://doi.org/10.1140/epjds/s13688-025-00523-3) | Market-wide volatility; intraday S&P company data. | Rolling correlation snapshots, autoencoder reconstruction, downstream forecasts; node and rewired-edge ablations. | Rewired edges retain value in some comparisons; basic graph randomization is already studied. | Verify timestamp alignment for graph score and outcome before replication. Need matched controls and avoid inferring causality. |
| Kumar et al., [TemporalGAT](https://doi.org/10.3390/math14020289) | Daily volatility proxies of eight international market indices. | LSTM + graph layers; fixed versus dynamically re-estimated spillover graphs. | Dynamic graph did not systematically outperform fixed graph in the stricter reported test. | Partition-specific graph descriptions merit implementation/timing verification; this is not proof of leakage. Index-node task differs from stock-node task. |
| Lee and Cho, [H-ETE-GNN](https://doi.org/10.3390/fractalfract9060339) | Daily national ETF returns; rolling-volatility proxy. | Pearson, Granger, TE and ETE graphs; Hurst-triggered graph rebuild and retraining. | Adaptation helps some aggregate comparisons but not every metric/year. | Verify data selection, scaling and threshold timing; rolling targets overlap. Regime-triggered retraining is already studied. |
| Bracht et al., [financial graph placebo controls](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=7154273) | Short-horizon equity-direction forecasting, according to abstract; preprint under review when identified. | Financial graphs and randomized graph/label placebo ablations. | Graph placebo tests are not new. | Read full preprint and verify version, data, controls and status before positioning our contribution. |
| Wang et al., [systemic risk and connectedness](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.irfa.2024.103147) | Chinese financial-sector risk, per abstract and publisher sections. | DCC-GARCH network and ML comparisons. | Connectedness-risk forecasting is not new. | Full text not obtained; do not claim detailed omissions. |
| Song et al., [network risk indicators](https://researchportal.hkust.edu.hk/en/publications/on-the-predictive-power-of-network-statistics-for-financial-risk-/) | International-market risk indicators, per institution record / abstract. | Time-varying network statistics. | Network statistics for risk prediction are not new. | Full text not obtained; do not infer exact controls or split from abstract. |
| Corsi, [HAR-RV](https://doi.org/10.1093/jjfinec/nbp001) | Volatility over different time scales. | Heterogeneous autoregressive realized-volatility model. | A simple multiscale baseline can be strong. | Distinguish a daily-data HAR-inspired binary classifier from exact HAR-RV. |
| Feng et al., [temporal relational stock ranking](https://arxiv.org/abs/1809.09441) | Stock ranking / returns. | Temporal stock relationships and graph convolution. | Temporal relational learning in finance predates our proposal. | Ranking and market-stress prediction have different targets and metrics. |
| [Entropy-regularised graph forecasting](https://www.mdpi.com/1099-4300/28/7/756) | Multi-asset volatility / allocation, per article abstract. | Dynamic graph and uncertainty. | Uncertainty-aware financial graph methods already exist. | Read full paper and compare uncertainty and calibration method before any novelty claim. |

Next: expand citations in a targeted search, read full texts individually, check related prior event-triggered methods and stability-aware gating, and document source access. Do not present a potential gap as a verified claim until this matrix supports it.
